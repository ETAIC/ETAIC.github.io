description: "**Yisen Li** is a Research Assistant at the [ETAIC Lab](https://etaic.github.io/) (Embodied Technology for Autonomy, Intelligence, and Control) at the University of Texas at Arlington, working with [Prof. H. Eric Tseng](https://www.nae.edu/248787/Dr-Hongtei-Eric-Tseng), a member of the U.S. National Academy of Engineering, and Dr. Hao Zhang. He is currently pursuing his M.S.E. degree in Electrical Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania. He has also conducted research at the [Safe AI Lab](https://safeai-lab.github.io/) at Carnegie Mellon University.

His research focuses on safe and reliable robot learning for physical interaction, with particular interests in humanoid whole-body control, safe and interpretable multi-agent reinforcement learning, and human–robot collaboration. His recent work includes developing whole-body control frameworks and safety-aware multi-agent learning algorithms for collaborative transportation, with real-world validation on Unitree G1 humanoid robots. His broader research interests lie in advancing adaptive, trustworthy, and physically capable robotic systems for real-world applications.
