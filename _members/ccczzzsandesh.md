description: "Sandesh Thapa is a Visiting Researcher in the ETAIC Lab at the University of Texas at Arlington, hosted by Dr. H. Eric Tseng, where he works at the intersection of safety-critical control and reinforcement learning for robotic systems. He holds an M.S. in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering from Oklahoma State University, with a specialization in control theory and robotics. At OSU, he developed novel coordination, concurrent learning, and adaptive control algorithms for cooperative robotic systems. His M.S. research was awarded Best Thesis at the OSU Research Symposium in 2018 and led to five first-author publications (three IEEE conferences and two journals). He worked at the Drone Racing League as a Senior Flight Controls Research Engineer, developing autonomous control and planning algorithms. He also worked at Ford Research and Advanced Engineering, developing nonlinear control and path planning algorithms for autonomous vehicles. Dr. Eric Tseng was one of his mentors during his time at Ford. Recently, he held tech lead and Senior Research Engineer roles at Amogy, where he developed safety-critical control algorithms with a team of eight engineers. His research interests are in the design of safe control and decision-making algorithms for robotics and autonomous systems, utilizing mathematical properties of dynamical systems, control theory, and machine learning. He is interested in designing novel constrained-optimization-based algorithms that adapt dynamically, guarantee safety and are intuitive to implement in real time systems.
